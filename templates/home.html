BUILT WITH THE ASSUMPTIONS SHOWING

Confidence comes
from clarity.

No secret score. No invented performance.
Just historical calculations you can inspect.

01 Real history. Repeatable results. +

Historical Yahoo Finance data is cached locally for exact-request reuse. The saved example works offline once its stock and benchmark data are prepared. Retrieval failures are explicit; no generated prices are substituted.

02 A common starting line. +

Current, alternative and benchmark series use matching observed dates and start at an index of 100. At least 252 common observations are required. Missing quotes are not forward-filled, and coverage exclusions are disclosed.

03 Buy and hold. Read the fine print. +

Fractional initial weights; no rebalancing, cash flows, fees, taxes or slippage. Adjusted prices are a vendor return proxy, not actual account cash flows or fund NAVs. Past results do not establish future returns.

04 AI-assisted. Not AI-decided. +

Gemini interpretation is optional and user initiated. Numerical sourcing is validated against calculated server facts; qualitative interpretation is not independently certified. The historical workspace remains useful without a Gemini key.
