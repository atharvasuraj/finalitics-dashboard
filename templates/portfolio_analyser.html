Explore an illustrative stock allocation using saved 2022–2024 adjusted prices. This demonstration runs offline after data preparation.

Download sample CSV

Columns: Symbol,Weight. Supported stocks: RELIANCE.NS, HDFCBANK.NS, INFY.NS. The .NS suffix is optional.

Weights are nonnegative percentages totaling 100. Totals within 0.01 percentage points are scaled to exactly 100; other totals are rejected.
